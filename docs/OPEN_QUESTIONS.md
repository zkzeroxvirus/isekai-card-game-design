# Open Design Questions

Version: **v0.1.0**

These items are intentionally unresolved and should be answered through prototyping rather than assumed to be final rules.

## Combat

1. Does the player draw 5 new cards each turn or refill to a 5-card hand?
2. Is 3 Action the correct default economy?
3. Should unused Action disappear, partially carry over, or convert into another resource?
4. How many nested Reactions are acceptable before timing becomes too slow?
5. Should Guard always clear at start of turn, or do some classes retain a portion by default?

## Deck Construction

6. Is a 15-card minimum large enough to prevent degenerate consistency while still feeling like a character loadout?
7. Should decks have a hard maximum size?
8. Should class composition impose minimum card counts per equipped class?
9. How are Neutral cards limited so they do not erase class identity?
10. Are Signature cards mandatory, optional, or granted outside normal deck slots?

## Multiclassing

11. Is Primary/Secondary class distinction purely structural, or does Primary grant stronger passive access?
12. How many classes can one late-game character equip without destroying class identity?
13. Are Hybrid cards discovered automatically from mastery or through hidden achievements/events?
14. Can hidden classes coexist with their prerequisite classes?

## Status Effects

15. Finalize Bleed behavior.
16. Finalize Poison timing and removal.
17. Finalize Stagger threshold/break behavior.
18. Determine whether Burn decay of 1 per trigger is sufficiently scalable.
19. Define standard Weak and Vulnerable values/durations.

## Dungeon Runs

20. What is the target length of a normal run?
21. How much information about future rooms is visible by default?
22. What persists after defeat versus successful extraction?
23. Do players select a depth directly, select a range, or advance sequentially?
24. How frequently should Dungeon Laws appear before they feel routine rather than special?
25. What numerical scaling formula keeps earlier enemies relevant at extreme depth?

## Progression

26. Is character level separate from class mastery?
27. Can attributes be respecced freely, at a cost, or not at all?
28. How many active Treasure slots should the player begin with?
29. How many persistent currencies can the town support before progression becomes cluttered?
30. Which rewards are account-wide versus character-specific?

## Town

31. Is town progression shared across all characters?
32. Can the player specialize buildings into mutually exclusive branches?
33. Should town NPC relationships be a mechanical progression layer?
34. Which building unlocks multiclassing?
35. Which building controls deck editing, if deck editing is ever restricted?

## Format / Product Direction

36. Is the intended game primarily digital, physical, or digitally assisted tabletop?
37. Is combat single-character, party-based, or does one player control a primary character plus summons/companions?
38. Is this solo-first, cooperative, competitive, or capable of multiple formats?
39. How much hidden information should exist in combat?
40. What is the intended average complexity of a single card at early, mid, and late progression?

## Next Prototype Decision Set

The highest-priority questions for a playable paper/digital mockup are **1, 2, 6, 11, 15–18, 20, 22, and 36–38**.

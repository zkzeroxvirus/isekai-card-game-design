# Equipment Progression

Version: **v0.2.0**

## Purpose

Equipment is a persistent character-building layer with its own identity, mastery, abilities, and evolution paths. Gear should create playstyles and unlock possibilities rather than exist only as a stat ladder.

## Equipment Structure

An equipment item may contain:

- base item type
- quality tier
- equipment tags
- attribute modifiers
- affixes
- granted card or cards
- passive trait
- mastery track
- evolution conditions
- sockets or modifications
- durability state, where applicable

## Core Rule: Equipment Can Teach Skills

Using equipment may unlock cards, passives, proficiencies, or permanent knowledge associated with that item.

An item can therefore be valuable even after the player finds statistically stronger gear.

Example:

### Old Tower Shield

Initial effects:
- increases defensive capacity
- grants access to **Brace**

Mastery milestones:
- Block 100 damage → unlock **Shield Rush**
- Protect a companion from lethal damage → unlock **Interpose**
- Block 500 Elite/Boss damage → reveal evolution condition

Possible evolution:

### Bulwark of the Fallen Gate

- stronger Guard interactions
- grants a unique defensive stance
- modifies Shield-family skill evolution options

## Quality Tiers

Prototype quality vocabulary:

1. Common
2. Fine
3. Rare
4. Epic
5. Legendary
6. Mythic

Quality should influence affix potential, unusual mechanics, mastery depth, and crafting complexity. It should not guarantee that a higher-quality item is always correct for every build.

## Equipment Categories

Potential categories include:

- Main Hand
- Off Hand
- Two-Handed Weapon
- Armor
- Head
- Hands
- Feet
- Accessory
- Focus
- Tool
- Charm

Exact slot count remains a prototype balance question.

## Weapon Families

Weapons should support distinct technique families.

Examples:

- Sword
- Greatblade
- Spear
- Axe
- Hammer
- Dagger
- Bow
- Shield
- Staff
- Tome
- Wand
- Gauntlet

Weapon proficiency may affect what Techniques can be equipped or how they evolve.

## Equipment Mastery

Equipment mastery can advance through:

- defeating meaningful enemies while equipped
- using granted equipment cards
- performing item-specific actions
- completing crafting or repair milestones
- exposing equipment to relevant materials or environments
- fulfilling hidden conditions

Mastery rewards may include:

- permanent skill knowledge
- new equipment cards
- affix slots
- passive upgrades
- reduced restrictions
- evolution branches
- lore revelations

## Equipment Evolution

Some equipment can transform when conditions are met.

Evolution should usually require a combination of:

- mastery
- a thematic achievement
- material or boss component
- class or skill compatibility
- town crafting access

Example evolution tree:

**Apprentice Staff**
→ **Elemental Conduit**
→ Fire / Frost / Storm specialization

or

→ **Arcane Conduit**
→ spell-copy / mana-manipulation specialization

## Affixes

Affixes should alter build behavior where possible.

Examples:

- **Mana-Conductive** — equipment-generated attacks interact with Mana.
- **Bloodbound** — gains effects when Health is spent.
- **Goblin-Bane** — specialized monster-hunting property.
- **Echoing** — first granted Technique may repeat under a condition.
- **Warding** — modifies Guard or status resistance.

Flat-stat affixes may exist but should not dominate the most memorable loot.

## Crafting and Reforging

Town crafting may support:

- repair
- socketing
- rerolling or replacing affixes
- upgrading quality within limits
- binding monster materials
- awakening dormant properties
- evolving eligible gear

Crafting should create targeted agency without eliminating excitement from dungeon drops.

## Durability and Damage

**Prototype Direction:** Defeat may damage equipped gear rather than permanently destroy it.

Damaged equipment can create a meaningful recovery cost while preserving long-term character investment.

Permanent destruction should be reserved for explicit challenge modes or special mechanics, not normal play.

## Equipment vs Treasure

- **Equipment** belongs to the character's loadout and primarily enables or modifies character capabilities.
- **Treasures** are rare persistent rule-bending artifacts with limited slots and broader system effects.

## Design Principle

**Gear is part of the character's history. A favored item should be able to grow with the player instead of becoming vendor trash after three floors.**

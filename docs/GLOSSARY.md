# Controlled Glossary

Version: **v0.1.0**

Use these terms consistently in rules and card text.

## Action
Primary per-turn resource used to play many cards.

## Achievement
Persistent condition-based milestone that may unlock content.

## Affix
Reusable modifier applied to an enemy or encounter.

## Barrier
A defensive layer distinct from Guard; exact behavior is defined by its source.

## Card Type
Mechanical identity of a card, such as Technique, Spell, Reaction, Stance, Summon, Skill, Item, Ultimate, Curse, Blessing, or Event Card.

## Class
A modular discipline that grants access to cards, passives, permissions, and mastery rewards.

## Class Mastery
Persistent progression within a class.

## Deck
The set of cards currently equipped for play, plus temporary run changes where applicable.

## Discover
Choose one option from a generated subset of eligible options.

## Dungeon Law
A high-impact dungeon-wide or floor-wide rule that changes normal assumptions.

## Exhaust
Move a card to the Exhaust zone after resolution or when instructed. It normally cannot be drawn again during that combat.

## Guard
Temporary defense that absorbs incoming damage before Health.

## Hybrid Card
A card requiring a defined combination of classes, disciplines, tags, or mastery conditions.

## Innate
A card that begins combat in the opening hand, subject to setup rules.

## Known Card
A card permanently unlocked for deck construction.

## Origin
A persistent character background/archetype that grants starting identity outside the class system.

## Prepared
A special zone/state for cards intentionally held outside normal hand cycling.

## Primary Class
The character's principal equipped class slot.

## Reaction
A card played in response to a defined trigger.

## Retain
Keep a card in hand at end of turn instead of discarding it.

## Run Card
A temporary card gained for the current dungeon run.

## Secondary Class
An additional equipped class used for multiclass deck construction.

## Seal
Make a card inaccessible until a stated condition ends.

## Source
Where a card originated, such as Class, Hybrid, Neutral, Origin, Equipment, Treasure, Dungeon, Achievement, Title, Monster, Town, or Unique.

## Stance
A persistent card representing a combat mode or rule-changing posture.

## Tag
A descriptive label used for synergy, filtering, requirements, and content interactions. Tags do not automatically have rules text.

## Title
Persistent recognition earned through specific accomplishments; may be cosmetic or mechanically relevant.

## Treasure
Persistent major reward that may alter game rules and is distinct from normal equipment.

## Upgrade
A modification to a card, building, class, item, or other progression object.

## Rules Language Conventions

- Use **gain** for receiving Guard, resources, statuses, cards, or values.
- Use **deal damage** for an effect causing damage to another unit.
- Use **take damage** when a unit receives resolved damage.
- Use **draw** only for moving cards from Deck to Hand.
- Use **generate** for creating something that did not come from its normal zone.
- Use **discard** for moving from Hand to Discard without playing.
- Use **Exhaust** only when moving to the Exhaust zone.
- Use **defeat** instead of kill when referring to generic units unless narrative content intentionally requires another word.
- Use standardized trigger phrases from `MECHANICS.md` instead of synonyms.

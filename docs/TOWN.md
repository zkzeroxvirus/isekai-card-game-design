# Town Progression Framework

Version: **v0.1.0**

## Core Principle

The town is the persistent expression of the player's growth. Buildings should primarily unlock or deepen systems rather than merely provide passive percentage bonuses.

## Town Phase Functions

The Town Phase supports:

- character management
- deck construction
- class training
- equipment management
- crafting
- treasure management
- dungeon preparation
- quests and NPC interactions
- achievement review
- unlock discovery

## Building Upgrade Philosophy

A building level should preferably do one of the following:

1. unlock a new action,
2. expand meaningful choice,
3. improve information,
4. enable new content,
5. reduce friction on an already-understood system.

Flat bonuses can exist, but should not be the town's primary identity.

## Town Resources

Potential persistent resource categories:

- Gold — common currency
- Materials — construction and crafting
- Essence — magical upgrades
- Renown — guild/town progression
- Knowledge — research/discovery
- Boss Materials — specialized crafting/unlocks

Final resource count should be kept as low as possible. Every persistent currency must have a clear sink and acquisition identity.

## Town Growth

Town upgrades may require combinations of:

- dungeon depth milestones
- materials
- gold
- achievements
- NPC recruitment
- boss kills
- class mastery
- quests

Some buildings or upgrade branches may remain hidden until their conditions are discovered.

## Design Rule

Town upgrades must not make dungeon gameplay obsolete. They should open more preparation, experimentation, recovery, and content rather than convert the game into automatic passive scaling.

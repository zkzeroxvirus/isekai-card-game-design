# Hearthcross Job Ecosystem

**Design Version: v0.2.18**

## Purpose

Hearthcross is the first implementation of location-based Job discovery.

It is a frontier settlement, not a universal progression hub. Its Job ecosystem should therefore feel practical, limited, and rooted in the needs of the Greyfen March.

The settlement offers a small number of foundational Jobs, several modest local specializations, and a handful of hidden identities tied to the Greyfen, Underbridge, and The Buried Gate.

The intended rule is:

**Locations create opportunities. Actions create qualifications. The Codex recognizes the result.**

A second rule governs settlement progression:

**Settlement growth increases depth before breadth.**

Even a highly developed Hearthcross should become better at frontier survival, expeditions, logistics, Greyfen craft, medicine, scouting, and dungeon research rather than eventually teaching every profession in the game.

---

## Location-Based Job Discovery

Jobs should not come from a universal catalogue.

A location can expose Jobs through four sources:

1. **Institutions** — Guilds, temples, workshops, armies, schools, factions.
2. **Communities & Cultures** — local traditions, nonhuman peoples, criminal groups, clans.
3. **Activities & Environments** — repeated work, survival, exploration, crafting, travel.
4. **Secrets & Anomalies** — hidden conditions, dungeon phenomena, forbidden knowledge, unusual behavior.

A Job can require more than one source.

Example:

**Dungeon Surveyor** may require Survey Office access, actual dungeon mapping, hazard identification, and successful extraction with useful records.

---

# Hearthcross Initial Job Availability

At the beginning of the shared campaign, Hearthcross should offer only a small practical roster.

## Openly Discoverable Jobs

### 1. Adventurer

**Primary Source:** Adventurer Guild Hall

**Fantasy:** A licensed frontier problem-solver willing to accept danger for pay, reputation, discovery, or opportunity.

**Typical Qualification:**

- register with the Adventurer Guild
- complete initial evaluation or probationary contract
- demonstrate basic expedition capability

**Effects:**

- improved interpretation of Guild contract information
- modest expedition preparation benefits
- increased access to Guild recovery/logistics services

**Skills:**

- Contract Reading
- Expedition Preparation
- Basic Monster Lore
- Field Discipline

**Permissions:**

- accept regulated contracts
- use selected Guild facilities
- participate in official dungeon expeditions
- access Guild intelligence appropriate to rank

**Recognition:**

- Guild and Wardens recognize licensed status
- civilians may treat the character as a professional problem-solver

**Local Growth:**

Hearthcross can deepen Adventurer into frontier-oriented progression, but advanced specialist Jobs should generally require travel.

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Delver
- Bounty Hunter
- Expedition Leader
- Relic Seeker
- Monster Hunter

---

### 2. Scout

**Primary Source:** March Wardens / Greyfen patrol routes

**Fantasy:** Read terrain, identify danger, and keep others from walking blindly into trouble.

**Typical Qualification:**

- patrol Greyfen roads or perimeter routes
- identify tracks, ambush signs, or hazards
- successfully report useful field intelligence

**Effects:**

- improved detection of environmental danger
- improved travel encounter information
- reduced penalties from unfamiliar routes

**Skills:**

- Observation
- Tracking
- Fieldcraft
- Route Sense

**Permissions:**

- access selected Warden patrol information
- mark threats or routes on regional maps
- participate in reconnaissance contracts

**Recognition:**

- Wardens provide more detailed operational information
- caravans may seek scouting assistance

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Pathfinder
- Ranger
- Monster Tracker
- Border Warden
- Expedition Scout

---

### 3. Smith

**Primary Source:** The Split Anvil

**Fantasy:** Keep frontier equipment alive through repair, material knowledge, and practical metalwork.

**Typical Qualification:**

- assist with equipment repair
- correctly work Greyfen iron or common materials
- demonstrate Repair or Smithing proficiency

**Effects:**

- improved equipment condition assessment
- reduced material waste in basic repairs
- better understanding of mundane equipment failure

**Skills:**

- Repair
- Smithing
- Metallurgy
- Tool Handling

**Permissions:**

- perform selected field and forge repairs
- participate in equipment modification work
- inspect structural damage and material quality

**Recognition:**

- craftspeople respond as peers or apprentices depending on mastery
- Guild members may request repair work

**Hearthcross Limitation:**

The Split Anvil can teach practical frontier smithing. It cannot teach advanced royal metallurgy, master enchanting, exotic cultural forging, or high-order runecraft by default.

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Weaponsmith
- Armorsmith
- Runesmith
- Monster-Smith
- Siege Smith

---

### 4. Field Medic

**Primary Source:** Guild infirmary, Warden injuries, refugees, Lantern House emergencies

**Fantasy:** Keep people alive when ideal tools and safe conditions do not exist.

**Typical Qualification:**

- stabilize injured NPCs or players
- perform meaningful triage
- demonstrate Medicine or Anatomy-related capability

**Effects:**

- better wound assessment
- improved field stabilization
- reduced waste of common medical supplies

**Skills:**

- Medicine
- Triage
- Anatomy
- Bandaging

**Permissions:**

- perform advanced stabilization interactions
- assist in recovery events
- identify selected wounds, diseases, or toxins

**Recognition:**

- wounded NPCs and frontier institutions may seek assistance
- medical dialogue opens during crises

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Combat Medic
- Physician
- Apothecary
- Plague Doctor
- Chirurgeon

---

### 5. Trader

**Primary Source:** Hearthcross merchants / Fenroad Consortium

**Fantasy:** Understand scarcity, routes, value, and the people willing to move goods through danger.

**Typical Qualification:**

- complete multiple meaningful trades
- identify a local shortage or surplus
- profit through legitimate exchange rather than raw loot selling alone

**Effects:**

- better price/context information
- improved recognition of regional shortages
- greater access to trade opportunities

**Skills:**

- Appraisal
- Negotiation
- Market Sense
- Logistics

**Permissions:**

- participate in trade contracts
- invest in selected caravan opportunities
- identify economic opportunities in world-state changes

**Recognition:**

- merchants provide different dialogue and opportunities
- Consortium attitudes become more nuanced

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Caravaner
- Broker
- Appraiser
- Quartermaster
- Black-Market Trader

---

### 6. Survey Assistant

**Primary Source:** Survey Office / Archive Annex

**Fantasy:** Record what the frontier does not yet understand.

This is intentionally humbler than Scholar or Archaeologist.

**Typical Qualification:**

- assist with maps, samples, records, or recovered inscriptions
- return useful expedition observations
- demonstrate Observation, Research, or Cartography capability

**Effects:**

- improved documentation of discovered sites
- improved interpretation of basic ruin/terrain information
- increased Knowledge gain from properly recorded discoveries

**Skills:**

- Research
- Cartography
- Documentation
- Pattern Recognition

**Permissions:**

- submit field records
- access selected Survey Office archives
- annotate regional discoveries

**Recognition:**

- scholars and surveyors treat the player as a useful field contributor

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Cartographer
- Archivist
- Archaeologist
- Dungeon Surveyor
- Ruin Scholar

---

## Semi-Hidden / Relationship-Based Jobs

These Jobs exist in Hearthcross but should not be advertised to every player.

### 7. Acolyte of the Returning Flame

**Primary Source:** Shrine of the Returning Flame

**Discovery:** Relationship / ritual participation rather than job-board selection.

**Typical Qualification:**

- assist shrine work or rites
- demonstrate knowledge or sympathy toward Returning Flame doctrine
- participate meaningfully in funerary, protective, or restorative acts

**Effects:**

- basic recognition of Returning Flame rites
- improved resistance/interpretation in selected spiritual contexts
- small benefits when assisting shrine recovery or funerary work

**Skills:**

- Theology
- Ritual Practice
- Spirit Lore
- Consolation

**Permissions:**

- assist in rites
- access restricted shrine dialogue
- participate in spiritual investigations

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Priest
- Exorcist
- Flame Keeper
- Inquisitor
- Heretic (through divergence rather than direct promotion)

---

### 8. Smuggler

**Primary Source:** Underbridge Network

**Discovery:** Hidden until the player earns criminal trust or performs qualifying behavior.

**Typical Qualification:**

- move restricted goods, information, or people without official approval
- evade or manipulate inspection
- build Underbridge trust

**Effects:**

- improved contraband recognition
- better understanding of unofficial routes and fences
- contextual benefits to covert logistics

**Skills:**

- Deception
- Concealment
- Street Sense
- Illicit Logistics

**Permissions:**

- access restricted Underbridge trade
- use selected covert routes
- participate in smuggling events

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Fence
- Contraband Runner
- Informant
- Underworld Broker
- Black-Market Trader

# Greyfen Activity Jobs

These are not primarily taught by town institutions. They emerge through what the player repeatedly does in the surrounding region.

## 9. Forager

**Source:** Greyfen wilderness

**Qualification Examples:**

- identify useful plants/materials
- gather without destroying valuable sources
- survive multiple field excursions using gathered resources

**Effects:**

- improved common resource identification
- reduced waste from gathering
- basic field-use recipes or preparation knowledge

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Herbalist
- Field Alchemist
- Poison Gatherer
- Wild Cook

Hearthcross can support only the basic Job and limited local refinement.

---

## 10. Trapper

**Source:** Greyfen routes, wildlife pressure, Warden work

**Qualification Examples:**

- construct or use meaningful traps
- capture or redirect dangerous wildlife
- understand animal paths and behavior

**Effects:**

- improved trap placement and interpretation
- stronger field-control options outside direct combat

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Hunter
- Beaststalker
- Monster Trapper
- Saboteur

---

# Buried Gate Jobs

The first dungeon should introduce Jobs that feel impossible to acquire safely from town life alone.

## 11. Dungeon Surveyor

**Availability:** Initially hidden or partially visible.

**Sources:** Survey Office + The Buried Gate

**Qualification Examples:**

- map multiple dungeon branches
- identify hazards correctly
- record structural anomalies
- return with useful information rather than merely loot
- successfully extract with survey records

**Effects:**

- improved dungeon route annotation
- increased recognition of repeated room structures and hazards
- additional contextual information at route decisions

**Skills:**

- Cartography
- Dungeon Sense
- Structural Observation
- Hazard Analysis

**Permissions:**

- file official dungeon surveys
- unlock specialized expedition contracts
- contribute to future town knowledge upgrades

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Deep Delver
- Ruin Cartographer
- Dungeon Architect Scholar
- Anomaly Surveyor

---

## 12. Relic Seeker

**Availability:** Hidden at first.

**Sources:** Buried Gate recoveries + Survey Office / Underbridge / Guild depending behavior

**Qualification Examples:**

- recover multiple unusual objects
- correctly recognize an object as more than vendor trash
- investigate provenance
- preserve at least one valuable relic for study instead of immediate profit

Alternative darker route may allow the same identity through illicit acquisition and resale.

**Effects:**

- improved recognition of unusual item provenance
- better detection of hidden relic properties
- stronger contextual choices around recovered artifacts

**Future Branch Seeds:**

- Archaeologist
- Relic Appraiser
- Treasure Hunter
- Forbidden Curator

# Hidden Hearthcross Jobs

These should reward character history rather than appear in normal progression UI.

## Hidden Job — Greyfen Guide

**Theme:** The frontier begins trusting you to bring people through places that kill the unprepared.

Possible qualifications:

- Scout or Survey Assistant progression
- safely escort multiple groups through Greyfen routes
- identify hazards before they trigger
- discover at least one alternate route
- avoid abandoning endangered travelers during qualifying expeditions

Potential effects:

- improved group travel preparation
- better prediction of Greyfen encounter risks
- contextual route options during escorts

This Job is a precursor to Pathfinder or Expedition Guide rather than an end state.

---

## Hidden Job — Gate Listener

**Theme:** You noticed that the Buried Gate is not merely architecture, and something about your repeated attention has begun to receive an answer.

Possible qualifications:

- Mana Sensitivity, System Comprehension, Research, or equivalent perceptive capability
- repeatedly investigate Gate geometry/anomalies
- experience the first Gate recognition event
- interact with anomalous structures without immediately destroying or exploiting all of them
- return to study the phenomenon after the first expedition

Potential effects:

- occasional perception of Gate resonance
- improved recognition of Buried Gate architecture
- hidden qualification progress toward future Gate-related Jobs/Disciplines

**Important:** Gate Listener should not explain the mystery. It should deepen it.

Possible later branches:

- Gate Scholar
- Anomaly Surveyor
- Gatekeeper-like classifications much later

---

## Hidden Job — Fen Scavenger

**Theme:** Survival through salvage, improvisation, and extracting value from things civilized professions discard.

Possible qualifications:

- frequent salvage of damaged equipment/materials
- Improvised Tools or Repair capability
- use recovered parts instead of purchasing replacements
- meaningful interaction with Greyfen goblin scavenger culture

Potential effects:

- improved salvage yields from broken mundane objects
- better improvised repair options
- recognition from certain goblin or Underbridge groups

Possible branches:

- Scrapwright
- Salvage Smith
- Relic Scrapper

This creates the first seed for learning a Job tradition that may not originate from human institutions.

---

## Hidden Job — Oathbearer

**Theme:** Repeatedly accepting responsibility without institutional reward causes the Codex to classify the character's behavior as a role.

Possible qualifications:

- repeated protection/escort behavior
- fulfill promises despite losing profit or convenience
- refuse at least one opportunity to abandon a responsibility for a better reward
- Guardian, Adventurer, Acolyte, or no formal prerequisite depending route

Potential effects:

- stronger recognition of vows/promises
- selected dialogue and hidden Guardian/faith progression hooks

Possible branches:

- Oathkeeper
- Last Bastion-related routes
- sacred or secular protector identities

This is an example of a Job strongly dependent on **negative conditions and behavior** rather than grinding an activity.

# Hearthcross Expansion Model

Hearthcross should have a few mutually competing or complementary development projects.

The player cannot instantly build every branch, and world state can determine which experts arrive or remain.

## Guild Expansion — Expedition Desk

Possible triggers:

- successful Buried Gate expeditions
- increased Guild influence
- safe road conditions

Adds depth to:

- Adventurer
- Dungeon Surveyor
- Scout

Can introduce:

- expedition logistics training
- more advanced extraction contracts
- early Delver qualification hints

Does **not** create full high-rank Guild specialization locally.

---

## Split Anvil Expansion — Frontier Forge

Possible triggers:

- recovered Buried Gate metalwork
- support the blacksmith
- reliable material supply

Adds depth to:

- Smith
- equipment mastery
- Greyfen-specific repairs

May reveal:

- primitive rune-compatible techniques
- Gatewarden Shield / Gate-Split Blades evolution support

Does **not** replace a true runic academy or master forge elsewhere.

---

## Survey Office Expansion — Field Archive

Possible triggers:

- submit dungeon maps and samples
- protect/recruit a capable researcher
- invest recovered knowledge instead of selling everything

Adds depth to:

- Survey Assistant
- Dungeon Surveyor
- Relic Seeker
- Gate Listener

Can unlock better Knowledge interfaces and research projects.

Does **not** become a major university.

---

## Shrine Expansion — Hospice & Spirit House

Possible triggers:

- support refugees/injured
- resolve spiritual events
- strengthen Returning Flame presence

Adds depth to:

- Field Medic
- Acolyte
- spirit-related Skill discovery

May introduce:

- funeral specialist
- basic exorcism training

Can create tension with Underbridge or forbidden research paths depending world state.

---

## Underbridge Expansion — Hidden Routes

Possible triggers:

- strengthen Underbridge Network
- reduce Warden control or negotiate coexistence
- complete covert logistics work

Adds depth to:

- Smuggler
- Trader
- Fen Scavenger
- illicit Relic Seeker routes

May introduce:

- Fence-like qualification
- forbidden materials
- criminal contacts from outside Greyfen

This should carry consequences rather than functioning as a free bonus district.

---

# Expansion Tradeoffs

Hearthcross growth should not be a simple checklist where everything becomes maximally developed.

Examples:

- expanding shrine authority may reduce openly available forbidden research
- strengthening Wardens may make smuggling harder while improving Scout opportunities
- empowering Underbridge may increase illicit Job depth while weakening formal merchant confidence
- selling all Buried Gate artifacts funds immediate town growth but slows Survey Office/Gate research
- integrating goblin scavengers may unlock Scrapwright/Fen Scavenger paths while angering factions that want them expelled

These do not always need to be binary lockouts, but they should create meaningful differences between host timelines.

# Location Job Template for Future Regions

Every major location should answer:

1. What ordinary Jobs are openly teachable here?
2. What Jobs emerge from local activities/environment?
3. What Jobs belong to local cultures or factions?
4. What hidden Jobs can only be discovered here?
5. Which Jobs are merely precursor stages?
6. What specializations require leaving this location?
7. What world-state changes deepen or remove Job access?
8. Which teachers, communities, or institutions can die, leave, or be recruited?
9. Which Jobs create new narrative/world interactions rather than combat bonuses?
10. Which strange combinations can seed hidden Disciplines or Authorities later?

---

# Locked Principles

1. Jobs are location-, culture-, institution-, activity-, and secret-driven rather than universally listed.
2. Hearthcross intentionally offers a small practical frontier roster.
3. Hearthcross primarily teaches precursor Jobs rather than advanced global specializations.
4. Settlement growth increases depth before breadth.
5. Advanced Job specializations should often require travel to appropriate cultures, institutions, environments, or hidden locations.
6. Hearthcross Job availability changes with faction and world-state development.
7. Some Job opportunities can disappear if mentors, factions, cultures, or institutions are lost.
8. Dungeon activity can create Jobs that civilization alone cannot teach.
9. Nonhuman cultures can possess learnable Job traditions.
10. Hidden Jobs should be surprising but interpretable in hindsight.
11. Hearthcross expansion should create tradeoffs and distinct host timelines rather than eventually unlocking everything.
12. New regions should expand the character-build space by introducing new ways to become someone.

# Adventurer Guild & Rank

Version: **v0.2.0**

## Purpose

The Adventurer Guild is the world's institutional progression layer. It tracks reputation, contracts, access, and public recognition independently from raw character strength.

A powerful character can be low-ranked if they have not completed guild requirements. A respected veteran can hold a high rank without being the strongest combatant in the world.

## Core Rule: Guild Rank Is Not Character Level

Guild Rank represents proven reliability within the adventuring system.

Rank can gate:

- contracts
- dungeon permits
- restricted regions
- guild services
- trainer access
- companion recruitment
- merchant stock
- political or social recognition
- elite hunts
- world-scale requests

## Rank Advancement

Advancement should require multiple forms of proof rather than pure experience.

Possible requirements:

- completed contracts
- boss or elite kills
- minimum dungeon depth
- guild evaluation encounter
- reputation threshold
- rescue or escort success
- class certification
- recommendation from an NPC
- special examination

Higher ranks should increasingly require unusual accomplishments.

## Contracts

Guild contracts provide structured expedition goals.

Categories may include:

- Hunt
- Extermination
- Escort
- Rescue
- Exploration
- Recovery
- Investigation
- Suppression
- Gathering
- Emergency
- Raid

Contracts can modify dungeon objectives and reward structures.

## Reputation

Reputation is related to, but separate from, Guild Rank.

Possible reputation axes:

- Guild Standing
- Town Standing
- Faction Standing
- Regional Fame
- Infamy

This allows consequences for how the player solves problems rather than only whether they succeed.

## Certifications

Some mechanics may require certifications rather than level gates.

Examples:

- Advanced Dungeon Permit
- Monster Handling License
- Alchemical Handling Certification
- Summoner Registration
- High Magic Authorization
- Expedition Leader Certification

These create worldbuilding and mechanical gates without forcing every unlock through character level.

## Guild Records

The Guild records notable player accomplishments.

Records can feed:

- achievements
- titles
- bestiary research
- rank advancement
- bounty creation
- NPC reactions
- rival adventurer events

## Guild Rank Rewards

Rank advancement should primarily unlock opportunity.

Good rewards include:

- new contract tiers
- deeper dungeon access
- trainer access
- new companion pools
- guild facilities
- special merchants
- storage or logistics improvements
- prestige dialogue
- world events

Avoid making every rank a mandatory raw-stat bonus.

## Design Principle

**Character Level measures capability. Guild Rank measures what the world believes you have proven.**

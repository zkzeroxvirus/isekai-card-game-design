# v0.2.7 — Living World, Factions & Consequences

Date: **2026-08-13**

## Canon Principles Locked

- **The world remembers.**
- **Time creates consequences.**
- **Factions judge independently.**
- **Morality emerges from behavior rather than a Good/Evil button.**
- **Villainy is a supported progression path, not a failure state.**
- **Settlements visibly record campaign history.**
- **The host's timeline defines multiplayer world reality.**
- **The scope of decisions grows alongside the player's power.**

## Added — World Time

- World time advances through meaningful actions such as travel, rest, expeditions, recovery, training, crafting, and major narrative events.
- Time-sensitive situations may progress while the player pursues other goals.
- World time exists to create consequences and opportunities rather than punish exploration.

## Added — Host-Owned Timeline

- The host world owns chronology, NPC state, settlement control, wars, faction power, regional threats, and persistent narrative outcomes.
- Visiting players enter the host's current timeline without replacing their own world state.

## Added — Faction Reputation

- Reputation is tracked independently for meaningful factions.
- Factions may evaluate trust, fear, respect, obligation, ideology, criminal standing, and religious standing.
- Players may simultaneously be admired by one faction and hated by another.
- Reputation can affect contracts, prices, access, guards, trainers, companions, hidden classes, cards, travel, political support, and divine attention.

## Added — Hidden Behavioral Tendencies

Prototype behavioral axes:

- Mercy ↔ Ruthlessness
- Order ↔ Freedom
- Selflessness ↔ Ambition
- Sacred ↔ Profane

These are behavioral records rather than an absolute morality score.

### Rule

**No choice exists only to fill an alignment meter.**

Choices should materially affect people, places, resources, factions, quests, relationships, or world conditions.

## Added — Consequence Records

Major decisions may persist as structured world-state records containing timestamps, affected NPCs/factions/regions, immediate outcomes, delayed hooks, reputation effects, and narrative tags.

This supports consequences returning much later in a campaign.

## Added — Faction Projects

Factions can independently pursue long-term projects such as:

- invasions
- rebellions
- trade routes
- rituals
- fortress construction
- tribal unification
- magical research
- artifact recovery

Players may assist, sabotage, ignore, redirect, negotiate, or exploit these projects.

## Added — Dynamic Settlements

Settlements may track population, security, prosperity, resources, faction control, magical stability, corruption, infrastructure, threats, refugees, and local reputation.

Locations may physically change due to player choices and faction activity.

## Changed — Player Town

The player town is now explicitly a visible campaign record.

Different worlds may develop into radically different forms based on alliances, religion, monster diplomacy, political choices, crimes, economic policy, and major quests.

## Added — Heroic, Villainous & Mixed Routes

The game supports:

- heroic
- villainous
- pragmatic
- opportunistic
- revolutionary
- tyrannical
- mixed-character trajectories

Villainous routes should unlock distinct companions, factions, classes, settlements, economies, and narrative arcs rather than merely destroy heroic content.

## Added — Companion Moral Agency

Companions may react according to their own values through approval, confrontation, loyalty changes, personal scenes, ideological development, departure, or betrayal.

No universal companion morality table is required.

## Added — State-Driven Narrative Events

Narrative events may be unlocked by combinations of:

- world time
- faction standing
- faction projects
- settlement state
- Origin
- Classes
- Skills
- Equipment
- Titles
- Bestiary Knowledge
- companions
- prior decisions
- behavioral tendencies

Character builds therefore continue to function as narrative keys.

## Added — World Eras

Provisional long-form campaign eras:

1. Arrival Era
2. Adventurer Era
3. Heroic Era
4. Sovereign Era
5. Mythic Era

Era advancement is based on narrative/world milestones rather than character level alone.

## Multiplayer Narrative Rule

**When visiting another player's world, the visitor experiences the host's consequences.**

Familiar locations, NPCs, wars, alliances, religions, economies, and faction relationships may therefore differ radically between host worlds.

## Design Guardrails

- Do not label every choice Good or Evil.
- Do not make villain routes reduced-content routes.
- Do not create severe irreversible consequences from trivial or poorly communicated choices.
- Telegraph major time-sensitive stakes where reasonable.
- Consequences may surprise the player but should not feel arbitrary.
- Factions are allowed to disagree morally about the same event.
- Political rulership is optional rather than mandatory.
- Solo, co-op, heroic, villainous, and morally mixed characters must all retain viable progression.

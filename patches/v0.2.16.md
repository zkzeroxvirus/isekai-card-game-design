# v0.2.16 — Duelist Mobile Melee Combat Package

## Added

- Third complete playable discipline: **Duelist**.
- Third prototype weapon package: **Twin Blades**.
- New discipline resource: **Flow**.
- Reliable Weapon Arts: Passing Cut, Rising Fang, Slipstep, and Reversal Cut.
- Narrow melee defense action: Deflect.
- Five starter manifestations: Pursuit, Perfect Tempo → Tempo Break, Open Vein, Echoing Steel, and Sever the Moment.
- Shared team state: **Opened**.
- First explicit Guardian ↔ Duelist and Arcanist ↔ Duelist synergy tests.
- First multiclass bridge concepts toward Vanguard/Blade Warden and Spellblade identities.
- Mastery branches for Passing Cut and Slipstep.
- Equipment evolution example: Hearthcross Twin Irons → Gate-Split Blades.

## Design Intent

Duelist fills the mobile offensive melee role between Guardian and Arcanist. The discipline should reward clean varied tempo, close-range footwork, angle changes, and exploiting short vulnerability windows rather than raw attack-speed spam.

The initial prototype combat triangle is now:

- **Guardian** — active defense, protection, stagger, Resolve.
- **Arcanist** — ranged setup, Sigils, control, Attunement.
- **Duelist** — mobility, precision, opening creation/exploitation, Flow.

These three packages should be used to validate whether the shared Weapon Art + Codex architecture supports substantially different playstyles and natural multiclass interactions.

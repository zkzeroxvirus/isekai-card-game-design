# v0.2.18 — Hearthcross Job Ecosystem

## Added

- First complete location-based Job implementation for Hearthcross and the Greyfen March.
- Core discovery rule: **Locations create opportunities. Actions create qualifications. The Codex recognizes the result.**
- Settlement progression rule: **Settlement growth increases depth before breadth.**
- Initial practical Hearthcross Job roster:
  - Adventurer
  - Scout
  - Smith
  - Field Medic
  - Trader
  - Survey Assistant
- Relationship/semi-hidden Jobs:
  - Acolyte of the Returning Flame
  - Smuggler
- Greyfen activity Jobs:
  - Forager
  - Trapper
- Buried Gate Jobs:
  - Dungeon Surveyor
  - Relic Seeker
- Hidden Hearthcross/Greyfen Jobs:
  - Greyfen Guide
  - Gate Listener
  - Fen Scavenger
  - Oathbearer
- Job availability tied to institutions, cultures, activities/environments, and secrets/anomalies.
- Hearthcross expansion projects that deepen rather than universalize the local Job ecosystem:
  - Guild Expedition Desk
  - Split Anvil Frontier Forge
  - Survey Office Field Archive
  - Shrine Hospice & Spirit House
  - Underbridge Hidden Routes
- World-state tradeoffs between Warden control, Underbridge power, shrine influence, research, goblin integration, and immediate economic gain.
- Future-region Job template for consistent location design.

## Design Intent

Hearthcross is intentionally limited. It should teach basic frontier identities and create prerequisite roots for later specializations without invalidating travel to major cities, cultural enclaves, academies, temples, specialist guilds, exotic biomes, or hidden locations.

A fully developed Hearthcross becomes exceptionally good at being Hearthcross; it does not become a universal capital.

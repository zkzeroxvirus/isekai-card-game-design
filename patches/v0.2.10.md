# v0.2.10 — Weapon Arts & Combat Layers

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Added

- Formalized three combat layers: Foundation Actions, Weapon Arts, and Codex Manifestations.
- Established the principle: **Weapons determine how you fight. Cards determine what extraordinary options you can manifest while fighting.**
- Added reliable weapon-family combat vocabularies independent of card draw.
- Defined the intended moveset formula: **Weapon Family + Class Discipline + Equipment Mastery = Weapon Moveset.**
- Established that equipment evolution may alter Weapon Arts and play patterns, not only statistics.
- Added a two-weapon-set Prototype Default for later testing.
- Formalized the five-slot real-time manifested hand as a control-slot model.
- Added standardized targeting families for manifestations.
- Established deck cycling as a candidate replacement/reduction for traditional cooldown dependence.
- Added card transformations and weapon/card interaction as primary combo structures.
- Added six-player combat-readability guardrails.

## Core Combat Architecture

1. Foundation Actions are reliable and non-random.
2. Weapon Arts are reliable actions defined by equipment and training.
3. Codex Manifestations provide rotating exceptional options.
4. Cards enhance and transform action combat rather than replacing basic combat.

## Prototype Defaults

- Five manifested hand slots.
- Up to two equipped weapon sets.
- Keyboard card-slot access through `1–5` as an initial layout.
- Controller supports a fast modifier layout with radial selection as an accessibility/preference alternative.

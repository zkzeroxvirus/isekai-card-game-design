# v0.2.17 — Jobs, Multiclassing & Hidden Progression

Date: 2026-08-13

## Added — Jobs

- Added Jobs as a progression layer separate from combat Disciplines.
- Jobs represent roles and identities the world/system recognizes the character as fulfilling.
- Standard Job structure now includes Job Effects, Job Skills, Job Permissions, Job Recognition, and Job Evolutions.
- Job progression comes from relevant activity rather than generic character XP.
- Characters may know many Jobs but only attune a limited number as Active Jobs at full benefit.
- Prototype progression begins at one Active Job slot, with additional slots becoming possible later.

## Added — Hidden Progression Framework

- Hidden progression is now a foundational reward structure across Jobs, Disciplines, Skills, Titles, Manifestations, equipment evolution, divine permissions, Treasures, and Authorities.
- Added condition families: Behavioral, Mastery, Narrative, World-State, Equipment, Failure, Combination, and Negative Conditions.
- Locked the rule that hidden unlocks should feel surprising but interpretable in hindsight.
- Added mutual exclusivity for selected hidden paths so character history creates real commitments.

## Added — System Comprehension Visibility Tiers

System Comprehension can now reveal hidden progression in stages:

- no explicit information or vague `Unknown Qualification Progressed`
- hidden-condition counts
- partial qualification identity/progress
- exact or alternate requirement visibility at exceptional comprehension

Learning how to discover secrets is itself progression.

## Added — Discipline Multiclassing Foundation

- Prototype direction remains Primary + Secondary Discipline.
- Both Disciplines should materially shape the build.
- Multiclassing can affect manifestation pools, Weapon Art modifications, resource interactions, Skill routes, hidden qualifications, and narrative identity.
- Multiclassing must produce integrated builds rather than merely larger card piles.

## Added — Hybrid Manifestations

Hybrid Manifestations are now formal abilities requiring meaningful integration across Disciplines and possibly Skills, Jobs, equipment, behavior, or narrative state.

Initial prototype bridges:

- Guardian + Duelist
- Arcanist + Duelist
- Guardian + Arcanist

## Added — Job/Discipline Interaction

Jobs can reshape Discipline progression and hybrid qualifications.

Examples include:

- Guardian + Priest
- Duelist + Assassin
- Arcanist + Scholar
- Arcanist + Blacksmith
- Guardian + Arcanist + Blacksmith
- Duelist + Arcanist + Hunter

Names such as Templar, Nightblade, Sage, Runesmith, Runic Bastion, and Arcane Stalker remain provisional examples rather than locked final content.

## Added — Otherworlder Job Flexibility

Otherworlders may become unusually flexible in perceiving, retaining, swapping, and combining Job classifications because of Divine Codex behavior.

This is not intended to make native characters automatically weaker. Native cultures may possess deep mastery, rare institutional Jobs, or hidden paths unavailable to an inexperienced otherworlder.

## Added — Endgame Classification Drift

Jobs can become increasingly conceptual at high progression, eventually blurring the distinction between profession, Title, metaphysical identity, and Authority.

Prototype progression ranges from ordinary Jobs such as Hunter or Blacksmith to conceptual classifications such as Gatekeeper, Worldwalker, Godslayer, or Lawbreaker.

## Prototype Target

Test:

- Primary + Secondary Discipline
- one Active Job slot
- four ordinary Jobs
- one hidden Job
- hidden Skill/manifestation recognition
- three first hybrid manifestation bridges
- System Comprehension visibility tiers
- one mutually exclusive path
- one negative-condition qualification
- one Hearthcross narrative interaction changed by Job
- one Job-modified equipment evolution route

# v0.2.8 — Narrative Structure

Date: 2026-08-13

## Narrative Direction Locked

The project now uses a **sandbox-first living world with a discoverable core mystery**.

The player may spend long periods pursuing personal goals, factions, town development, crafting, dungeons, companions, criminal activity, exploration, or regional stories without being forced through a rigid main-quest chain.

The Core Mystery exists to provide long-form direction and reveal the deeper truth behind the Divine Codex, Transference, Authorities, Dungeon Laws, the gods, and the Endless Dungeon.

## Added — Five Narrative Layers

1. **Core Mystery** — the deepest truths of the setting.
2. **Regional Storylines** — conflicts that can branch, evolve, or resolve without the player.
3. **Character & Companion Arcs** — persistent personal stories shaped by world state and relationships.
4. **Emergent World Stories** — consequences created by simulation, time, factions, and prior decisions.
5. **Player Campaign History** — the unique combination of all narrative layers plus player-created goals.

## Added — Story Revelations

- The Core Mystery advances primarily through discoveries rather than mandatory quest completion.
- Revelations may come from ancient inscriptions, Codex anomalies, otherworlders, forbidden archives, dead gods, corrupted manifestations, Authority fragments, scholars, deep Dungeon Laws, artifacts, and divine encounters.
- Compatible revelations can unlock new locations, conversations, events, and major Core Mystery choices.
- The intended feeling is that the player is **discovering the plot**.

## Added — No Constant Apocalypse Timer

- The Core Mystery does not use a permanent hidden global countdown that punishes ordinary sandbox play.
- Regional crises and faction projects may still advance, escalate, fail, or resolve with time.
- Core-story escalation is instead tied to World Era, discoveries, major world-state changes, faction actions, knowledge/power thresholds, and deliberate engagement.

## Added — Era-Based Narrative Escalation

Provisional campaign structure:

**Arrival → Adventurer → Heroic → Sovereign → Mythic**

Narrative scale grows from survival and local identity to regional conflicts, divine attention, nation-level consequences, and eventually Authorities, Transference, gods, and world laws.

## Added — Morally Open Core Story

The central story does not assume a heroic protagonist.

Characters may pursue the truth to:

- protect the world
- understand their own Transference
- gain power
- overthrow gods
- preserve divine authority
- seize control of the system
- free otherworlders
- stop or exploit soul transfer
- preserve the system because its destruction may be worse

Consequences, faction reactions, companion responses, and world states determine outcomes rather than a canonical Good/Evil ending split.

## Added — Narrative Keys

Character progression may unlock narrative options through:

- Origin
- Class
- mastered Skills
- equipment mastery
- Treasures
- Authorities
- Titles
- achievements
- Guild Rank
- faction reputation
- behavioral tendencies
- Bestiary and World Knowledge
- companions
- prior decisions
- world projects
- settlement state
- Codex anomalies

The character build is therefore both a combat configuration and a set of ways to understand and interact with the world.

## Added — Multiplayer Narrative Rule

The host timeline defines narrative reality.

Visitors can participate in the host's stories and may contribute narrative keys from their own characters, but their personal world-state decisions do not automatically overwrite the host timeline.

## Narrative Rules Locked

1. The game has a real Core Mystery.
2. The Core Mystery is discovery-driven rather than a rigid linear quest chain.
3. The world remains worth inhabiting when the player ignores the Core Mystery.
4. Regional stories can resolve without player intervention.
5. Companion and NPC arcs react to persistent state.
6. Emergent consequences are first-class narrative content.
7. Player campaign history is the personalized story.
8. Heroic and villainous characters can both pursue the central truth.
9. There is no permanent global apocalypse timer forcing continuous main-story progress.
10. Narrative scale grows with World Era.
11. Character builds provide narrative keys as well as combat capabilities.
12. Multiplayer narrative reality belongs to the host timeline.

## Deliberately Unresolved

- exact opening sequence
- first major Core Mystery revelation
- whether the protagonist is uniquely anomalous
- number and geography of major regions
- exact regional arcs
- final endgame choices
- whether the Core Mystery has one objective truth or multiple partially valid interpretations
- how campaign endings interact with postgame continuation

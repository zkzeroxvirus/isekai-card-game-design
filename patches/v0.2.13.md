# v0.2.13 — First Region & Dungeon

## Added — Greyfen March

Established the first shared frontier region reached after Origin-specific prologues.

The Greyfen March is a compact, reactive frontier sandbox built around trade, monster pressure, reclaimed land, ruins, and early dungeon exploration.

## Added — Hearthcross

Hearthcross is now the first shared settlement and multiplayer convergence point.

Key early locations include:

- Adventurer Guild Hall
- The Split Anvil blacksmith
- Lantern House inn/social hub
- Shrine of the Returning Flame
- Survey Office / Archive Annex
- Underbridge Market

The settlement is intentionally dense rather than geographically large and should remain relevant as the campaign develops.

## Added — First Regional Factions

Introduced the initial faction set:

- Hearthcross Adventurer Guild
- March Wardens
- Returning Flame clergy
- Fenroad Consortium
- Underbridge Network

These groups have compatible and conflicting interests rather than simple hero/villain alignment.

## Added — Early Living-World Seeds

The first region tracks persistent pressures such as:

- refugee population
- road safety
- goblin organization/hostility
- merchant confidence
- Warden control
- Guild influence
- dungeon activity
- shrine influence
- Underbridge strength

These can alter contracts, NPC presence, prices, patrols, settlement appearance, and later narrative content.

## Added — First Dungeon: The Buried Gate

The Buried Gate begins as a believable quarry/fortification ruin and transitions into architecture that does not fit local history or ordinary magical explanations.

The first run includes combat, traversal, hazards, a split route, Skill interactions, Bestiary learning, secured/unsecured loot, and a consequential goblin-scavenger encounter.

## Added — Gatebound Hob

The first boss is a goblin leader altered by prolonged exposure to the deeper dungeon structure.

Its second phase introduces a restrained reality/system anomaly so the first dungeon provides undeniable evidence that something deeper is operating beneath normal fantasy rules.

## Added — First Shared Story Revelation

After the boss, the party reaches the true Buried Gate.

Different Origins and System Comprehension levels may interpret it differently, but all share one fact:

**The Gate recognizes the party in some way.**

It does not fully open.

This is the first shared Core Mystery Story Revelation.

## Added — Nonbinary Dungeon Resolution

The goblin scavenger camp supports multiple outcomes including extermination, displacement, negotiation, trade, rescue, investigation, and betrayal.

Consequences can affect later encounters, Guild reports, Warden relations, criminal contacts, and future goblin relations.

No global morality meter is attached to the decision.

## Multiplayer

Open co-op begins after the personal Origin prologue and arrival at Hearthcross.

The host's regional state is authoritative, while visitors can contribute Origin-specific dialogue, Skills, System Comprehension, Knowledge, and faction history.

## Production Rule

**Prioritize density and consequence over acreage.**

The first region exists to prove the living-world, combat, narrative, dungeon, Origin, and multiplayer systems together before expanding world scale.
